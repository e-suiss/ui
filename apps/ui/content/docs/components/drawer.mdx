Drawer wraps the Base UI Drawer. The `Drawer` root manages the open state and the swipe direction, and `DrawerTrigger` opens it. `DrawerContent` renders the panel in a portal, over a dimmed `DrawerOverlay` when modal, with an optional swipe handle and close button. Inside it, `DrawerHeader` holds the `DrawerTitle` and `DrawerDescription`, and `DrawerFooter` holds the actions. `DrawerClose` closes the drawer from any element.

The drawer opens from the bottom by default and can also open from the top, left or right. It supports snap points, a floating style inset from the screen edges, and a non-modal mode. To get the iOS sheet effect, where the page behind scales down as a bottom drawer opens, wrap the page in `DrawerProvider` with `DrawerIndentBackground` and `DrawerIndent`. Use a drawer for touch-friendly, swipeable panels; for a centered window, use a dialog.

## Usage

```tsx
import { DrawerProvider, DrawerIndentBackground, DrawerIndent, Drawer, DrawerTrigger, DrawerPortal, DrawerClose, DrawerOverlay, DrawerSwipeHandle, DrawerContent, DrawerHeader, DrawerFooter, DrawerTitle, DrawerDescription } from "@/components/ui/drawer"

```

The drawer is uncontrolled by default; set `defaultOpen` to start open, or control it with `open` and `onOpenChange`. Set `swipeDirection` to `down`, `up`, `left` or `right` to choose the edge it opens from and the direction it is swiped to close.

Pass `snapPoints`, such as `[0.5, 1]`, to let people drag the drawer between heights; it opens at the first snap point unless you set `defaultSnapPoint`, or control it with `snapPoint` and `onSnapPointChange`. Set `modal={false}` to keep the rest of the page interactive; no overlay is rendered in that case.

<OnlyBase base="base aria">

Render the trigger or a close button as another component, such as a `Button`, with the `render` prop.

</OnlyBase>

<OnlyBase base="radix">

Set `asChild` on `DrawerTrigger` or `DrawerClose` to render your own `Button` as the trigger or close button.

</OnlyBase>

For the indented page effect, place `DrawerIndentBackground` and `DrawerIndent` inside `DrawerProvider` and put the page content in `DrawerIndent`. The page is scaled while a non-floating drawer that opens from the bottom is open, and, with snap points, only at the last snap point.

## Accessibility

- The panel has the `dialog` role. `DrawerTitle` provides its accessible name and `DrawerDescription` its accessible description.
- Opening the drawer moves focus inside it. Escape closes it and returns focus to the trigger.
- A non-modal drawer does not set `aria-modal`.
- The swipe handle is hidden from assistive technology, so always provide another way to close the drawer, such as a `DrawerClose` button.
- The icon-only close button has a visually hidden `Close` label.

## Notes

- Styling hooks: `data-slot` values such as `drawer-popup`, `drawer-content`, `drawer-overlay`, `drawer-swipe-handle`, `drawer-close-button`, `drawer-header`, `drawer-footer` and `drawer-indent`.
- The panel sets `data-swipe-direction`, `data-swipe-axis`, and, when applicable, `data-floating` and `data-snap-points`. A drawer at its last snap point gets `data-expanded`. `DrawerIndent` gets `data-raised` while the page is scaled.
- Side drawers are `75%` wide, and `24rem` wide from the `sm` breakpoint. Top and bottom drawers are limited to the viewport height minus `6rem`.
- Nested drawers stack, with the drawer behind scaled down slightly.
- Uses the `popover`, `background` and `border` colors, with a `bg-black/50` backdrop. Animations are disabled on `DrawerIndent` when reduced motion is preferred.

<Only platform="rn">

`Drawer` presents content in a panel attached to an edge of the screen. It slides in from the bottom by default, or from the top, left or right with `swipeDirection`, and the user can drag it closed in that direction. The content renders through a portal (inside a full-window overlay on iOS) and respects the safe-area insets.

Set `floating` to inset the panel from the screen edges with all corners rounded. Use `snapPoints` to let a bottom drawer rest at several heights. Wrap your app in `DrawerProvider` and its screen in `DrawerIndent` to scale the page back behind an open bottom drawer.

<Only platform="rn">

Compose `DrawerTrigger` and `DrawerContent` with a `DrawerHeader` (`DrawerTitle`, `DrawerDescription`), your content and a `DrawerFooter`. Use `DrawerClose` to close it from a button.

Set `showSwipeHandle` to show a grab handle, and `showCloseButton` on the content for a corner close button, with `closeLabel` for a text button. `snapPoints` accepts fractions of the screen such as `[0.4, 1]`; control the current one with `snapPoint` and `onSnapPointChange`.

With `modal={false}` there is no scrim. For the scaled background, render `DrawerIndentBackground` behind a `DrawerIndent` inside `DrawerProvider`.

</Only>

## API Reference

<Only platform="rn">

<ApiPart index={1}>

### DrawerProvider

Tracks open drawers for the indent effect.

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.ProviderProps`.

</ApiPart>

<ApiPart index={2}>

### DrawerIndentBackground

Background shown behind the scaled screen.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.IndentBackground>`.

</ApiPart>

<ApiPart index={3}>

### DrawerIndent

Screen wrapper that scales back while a bottom drawer is open.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.Indent>`.

</ApiPart>

<ApiPart index={4}>

### Drawer

Root that holds the open state, direction and snap points.

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `floating` | `boolean` | `false` | Insets the panel from the edges. |
| `showSwipeHandle` | `boolean` | `false` | Shows a grab handle. |

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.RootProps`.

</ApiPart>

<ApiPart index={5}>

### DrawerOverlay

Dimmed scrim behind a modal drawer.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.Backdrop>`.

</ApiPart>

<ApiPart index={6}>

### DrawerSwipeHandle

Grab handle on the dragging edge.

Also accepts the props of `ViewProps`.

</ApiPart>

<ApiPart index={7}>

### DrawerContent

Sliding panel with optional handle and close button.

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `portalHost` | `string` | - | Name of the portal host to render into. |
| `showCloseButton` | `boolean` | `false` | Shows a corner close button. |
| `closeLabel` | `string` | - | Text for the close button instead of the icon. |

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.Popup>`.

</ApiPart>

<ApiPart index={8}>

### DrawerHeader

Area for the title and description.

Also accepts the props of `ViewProps`.

</ApiPart>

<ApiPart index={9}>

### DrawerFooter

Area for actions at the end of the panel.

Also accepts the props of `ViewProps`.

</ApiPart>

<ApiPart index={10}>

### DrawerTitle

Drawer heading, centered for top and bottom drawers.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.Title>`.

</ApiPart>

<ApiPart index={11}>

### DrawerDescription

Supporting text.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.Description>`.

</ApiPart>

## Accessibility

- The popup has the `dialog` role and is labelled by the title.
- The swipe handle and decorative edge fill are hidden from screen readers.
- The icon close button is labelled "Close".

## Notes

- Padding follows the safe-area insets, and a docked drawer extends its background past the screen edge so drag overshoot shows no gap.
- Animations respect the system Reduce Motion setting.
- Uses `bg-popover`, `bg-black/50` and `bg-border` tokens; light and dark follow the system appearance.
