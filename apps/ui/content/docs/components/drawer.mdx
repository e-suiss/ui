Drawer wraps the Base UI Drawer. The `Drawer` root manages the open state and the swipe direction, and `DrawerTrigger` opens it. `DrawerContent` renders the panel in a portal, over a dimmed `DrawerOverlay` when modal, with an optional swipe handle and close button. Inside it, `DrawerHeader` holds the `DrawerTitle` and `DrawerDescription`, and `DrawerFooter` holds the actions. `DrawerClose` closes the drawer from any element.

The drawer opens from the bottom by default and can also open from the top, left or right. It supports snap points, a floating style inset from the screen edges, a non-modal mode and a page behind it that scales down. Use a drawer for touch-friendly, swipeable panels; for a centered window, use a dialog.

## Composition

Use the following composition to build a `Drawer`:

```text
Drawer
├── DrawerTrigger
└── DrawerContent
    ├── DrawerHeader
    │   ├── DrawerTitle
    │   └── DrawerDescription
    └── DrawerFooter
        └── DrawerClose
```

## Accessibility

- The panel has the `dialog` role. `DrawerTitle` provides its accessible name and `DrawerDescription` its accessible description.
- Opening the drawer moves focus inside it. Escape closes it and returns focus to the trigger.
- A non-modal drawer does not set `aria-modal`.
- The swipe handle is hidden from assistive technology, so always provide another way to close the drawer, such as a `DrawerClose` button.
- The icon-only close button has a visually hidden `Close` label.

## API Reference

### DrawerProvider

Coordinates drawers with `DrawerIndent` so the page can react when a drawer opens.

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.Provider.Props`.

Place `DrawerIndentBackground` and `DrawerIndent` inside it and put the page content in `DrawerIndent`.

### DrawerIndentBackground

The dark layer shown behind the page when it scales down.

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.IndentBackground.Props`.

### DrawerIndent

Wraps the page content and scales it down while a bottom drawer is open.

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.Indent.Props`.

The page is scaled while a non-floating drawer that opens from the bottom is open, and, with snap points, only at the last snap point. It gets `data-raised` while the page is scaled. Animations are disabled when reduced motion is preferred.

### Drawer

The root that manages the open state, swipe direction and snap points.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `floating` | `boolean` | `false` |
| `showSwipeHandle` | `boolean` | `false` |

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.Root.Props`.

The drawer is uncontrolled by default; set `defaultOpen` to start open, or control it with `open` and `onOpenChange`. With `snapPoints` it opens at the first snap point unless you set `defaultSnapPoint`, or control it with `snapPoint` and `onSnapPointChange`. `showSwipeHandle` shows a grab bar on the edge of the panel that faces the screen center. Nested drawers stack, with the drawer behind scaled down slightly.

### DrawerTrigger

The button that opens the drawer.

<OnlyBase base="radix">

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `asChild` | `boolean` | `false` |

</OnlyBase>

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.Trigger.Props`.

<OnlyBase base="base aria">

Render the trigger as another component, such as a `Button`, with the `render` prop.

</OnlyBase>

<OnlyBase base="radix">

Set `asChild` to render your own `Button` as the trigger instead of a `button`.

</OnlyBase>

### DrawerPortal

Renders its children into a portal outside the current DOM hierarchy.

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.Portal.Props`.

### DrawerClose

A button that closes the drawer.

<OnlyBase base="radix">

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `asChild` | `boolean` | `false` |

</OnlyBase>

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.Close.Props`.

<OnlyBase base="base aria">

Render the close button as another component, such as a `Button`, with the `render` prop.

</OnlyBase>

<OnlyBase base="radix">

Set `asChild` to render your own `Button` as the close button instead of a `button`.

</OnlyBase>

### DrawerOverlay

The dimmed layer behind a modal drawer, which fades as the drawer is swiped away.

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.Backdrop.Props`.

### DrawerSwipeHandle

The grab bar people drag to move or dismiss the drawer.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

### DrawerContent

The drawer panel, rendered in a portal with an optional overlay, swipe handle and close button.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `showCloseButton` | `boolean` | `false` |
| `closeLabel` | `string` | - |

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.Popup.Props`.

The panel sets `data-swipe-direction`, `data-swipe-axis`, and, when applicable, `data-floating` and `data-snap-points`. A drawer at its last snap point gets `data-expanded`. Side drawers are `75%` wide, and `24rem` wide from the `sm` breakpoint. Top and bottom drawers are limited to the viewport height minus `6rem`.

### DrawerHeader

Groups the title and description.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

### DrawerFooter

Holds the drawer's actions at the bottom of the panel.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

### DrawerTitle

The drawer's title, which also names it for assistive technology.

<OnlyBase base="radix">

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `asChild` | `boolean` | `false` |

</OnlyBase>

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.Title.Props`.

### DrawerDescription

Supporting text that describes the drawer for assistive technology.

<OnlyBase base="radix">

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `asChild` | `boolean` | `false` |

</OnlyBase>

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.Description.Props`.

Each part sets a `data-slot`, such as `drawer-content` or `drawer-swipe-handle`, for styling.

</Only>

Tracks open drawers for the indent effect.

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.ProviderProps`.

Wrap your app in it and render `DrawerIndentBackground` behind a `DrawerIndent` that holds the screen, to scale the page back behind an open bottom drawer.

### DrawerIndentBackground

Background shown behind the scaled screen.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.IndentBackground>`.

### DrawerIndent

Screen wrapper that scales back while a bottom drawer is open.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.Indent>`.

### Drawer

Root that holds the open state, direction and snap points.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `floating` | `boolean` | `false` |
| `showSwipeHandle` | `boolean` | `false` |

Also accepts the props of `DrawerPrimitive.RootProps`.

`showSwipeHandle` shows a grab handle. With `modal={false}` there is no scrim. A docked drawer extends its background past the screen edge so drag overshoot shows no gap, and animations respect the system Reduce Motion setting.

### DrawerOverlay

Dimmed scrim behind a modal drawer.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.Backdrop>`.

### DrawerSwipeHandle

Grab handle on the dragging edge.

Also accepts the props of `ViewProps`.

### DrawerContent

Sliding panel with optional handle and close button.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `portalHost` | `string` | - |
| `showCloseButton` | `boolean` | `false` |
| `closeLabel` | `string` | - |

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.Popup>`.

Set `showCloseButton` for a corner close button, and `closeLabel` to show text instead of the icon. `portalHost` names the portal host to render into. Padding follows the safe-area insets. Use `DrawerClose` to close the drawer from a button.

### DrawerHeader

Area for the title and description.

Also accepts the props of `ViewProps`.

### DrawerFooter

Area for actions at the end of the panel.

Also accepts the props of `ViewProps`.

### DrawerTitle

Drawer heading, centered for top and bottom drawers.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.Title>`.

### DrawerDescription

Supporting text.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof DrawerPrimitive.Description>`.
