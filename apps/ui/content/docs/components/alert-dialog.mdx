Alert Dialog wraps the alert dialog primitive of the chosen base. An `AlertDialog` root contains an `AlertDialogTrigger` and an `AlertDialogContent`. The content renders its own portal and scrim overlay, and typically holds an `AlertDialogHeader` (with optional `AlertDialogMedia`, plus `AlertDialogTitle` and `AlertDialogDescription`) followed by an `AlertDialogFooter` with `AlertDialogCancel` and `AlertDialogAction` buttons.

Use it when a decision must be made before continuing, such as signing out or deleting data. Unlike a regular dialog, it is not dismissed by clicking outside. For a choice of actions anchored to the bottom of the screen, use Alert Sheet.

## Composition

Use the following composition to build a `AlertDialog`:

```text
AlertDialog
├── AlertDialogTrigger
└── AlertDialogContent
    ├── AlertDialogHeader
    │   ├── AlertDialogTitle
    │   ├── AlertDialogDescription
    │   └── AlertDialogMedia
    └── AlertDialogFooter
        ├── AlertDialogCancel
        └── AlertDialogAction
```

## Accessibility

- The popup has the `alertdialog` role, named by `AlertDialogTitle` and described by `AlertDialogDescription`.
- Opening moves focus into the dialog; closing returns focus to the trigger.
- `Escape` closes the dialog.
- Clicking the overlay does not close it; a choice must be made with a footer button or `Escape`.

## API Reference

### AlertDialog

The root that manages the open state.

Also accepts the props of the primitive part it wraps.

<OnlyBase base="base radix">

The dialog is uncontrolled by default; use `defaultOpen`, or control it with `open` and `onOpenChange`.

</OnlyBase>

<OnlyBase base="aria">

The dialog is uncontrolled by default; use `defaultOpen`, or control it with `isOpen` and `onOpenChange`.

</OnlyBase>

### AlertDialogTrigger

The control that opens the dialog.

Also accepts the props of the primitive part it wraps.

### AlertDialogPortal

Renders the dialog into a portal outside the DOM hierarchy.

Also accepts the props of the primitive part it wraps.

### AlertDialogOverlay

The full-screen scrim behind the dialog.

Also accepts the props of the primitive part it wraps.

### AlertDialogContent

The dialog surface, rendered with its own portal and overlay.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `size` | `"default" \| "sm"` | `"default"` |

<OnlyBase base="aria">

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `isDismissable` | `boolean` | `false` |
| `isKeyboardDismissDisabled` | `boolean` | - |

`isDismissable` closes the dialog on a press outside it, and `isKeyboardDismissDisabled` stops Escape from closing it.

</OnlyBase>

Also accepts the props of the primitive part it wraps.

`AlertDialogContent` already includes `AlertDialogPortal` and `AlertDialogOverlay`, so you rarely use those parts directly. `size` sets the maximum width; `sm` is narrower than `default`.

<OnlyBase base="base">

Enter and exit use fade and zoom animations keyed to `data-open` and `data-closed`.

</OnlyBase>

<OnlyBase base="radix">

Enter and exit use fade and zoom animations keyed to `data-state="open"` and `data-state="closed"`.

</OnlyBase>

<OnlyBase base="aria">

Enter and exit use fade and zoom animations keyed to `data-entering` and `data-exiting`.

</OnlyBase>

### AlertDialogHeader

A centered layout wrapper for the media, title and description.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

### AlertDialogFooter

A two-column grid for the cancel and action buttons.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

### AlertDialogMedia

A circular container for an icon or image above the title.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

### AlertDialogTitle

The heading that names the dialog.

Also accepts the props of the primitive part it wraps.

### AlertDialogDescription

Supporting text that describes the dialog.

Also accepts the props of the primitive part it wraps.

### AlertDialogAction

A button that confirms and closes the dialog.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `"default" \| "outline" \| "secondary" \| "tinted" \| "ghost" \| "plain" \| "destructive" \| "link"` | `"default"` |
| `size` | `"default" \| "xs" \| "sm" \| "lg" \| "xl" \| "icon" \| "icon-xs" \| "icon-sm" \| "icon-lg" \| "icon-xl"` | `"default"` |

<OnlyBase base="base radix">

Also accepts the props of the primitive part it wraps.

</OnlyBase>

<OnlyBase base="aria">

Also accepts the props of [Button](/docs/components/button).

</OnlyBase>

`variant` and `size` are the `Button` variant and size. Use `variant="destructive"` for irreversible operations.

### AlertDialogCancel

A button that dismisses the dialog without acting.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `"default" \| "outline" \| "secondary" \| "tinted" \| "ghost" \| "plain" \| "destructive" \| "link"` | `"secondary"` |
| `size` | `"default" \| "xs" \| "sm" \| "lg" \| "xl" \| "icon" \| "icon-xs" \| "icon-sm" \| "icon-lg" \| "icon-xl"` | `"default"` |

<OnlyBase base="base radix">

Also accepts the props of the primitive part it wraps.

</OnlyBase>

<OnlyBase base="aria">

Also accepts the props of [Button](/docs/components/button).

</OnlyBase>

Both `AlertDialogCancel` and `AlertDialogAction` close the dialog and render a `Button`, styled with the `Button` `variant` and `size`. Each part sets a `data-slot`, such as `alert-dialog-content` or `alert-dialog-cancel`, for styling, and the content also sets `data-size`.

import SwiftUI

struct AlertDialogExample: View {
  var body: some View {
    AlertDialogDemo(title: "Show dialog", variant: .outline) {
      AlertDialogBody()
    }
  }
}

struct AlertDialogDemo<Body: View>: View {
  let title: LocalizedStringKey
  let variant: VariantButtonStyle.Variant
  let content: () -> Body

  @State private var open: Bool

  init(
    title: LocalizedStringKey,
    variant: VariantButtonStyle.Variant,
    open: Bool = false,
    @ViewBuilder content: @escaping () -> Body
  ) {
    self.title = title
    self.variant = variant
    self.content = content
    _open = State(initialValue: open)
  }

  var body: some View {
    Button(title) { open = true }
      .buttonStyle(.variant(variant))
      .alertDialog(isPresented: $open, content: content)
  }
}

  }

## API Reference

<Only platform="rn">

### AlertDialog

The root that manages the open state.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `open` | `boolean` | - |
| `defaultOpen` | `boolean` | `false` |
| `onOpenChange` | `(open: boolean, details) => void` | - |

Use `open` and `onOpenChange` for controlled state, or `defaultOpen` for uncontrolled. `onOpenChange` is called when the dialog opens or closes.

### AlertDialogOverlay

Dimmed scrim behind the dialog.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof AlertDialogPrimitive.Backdrop>`.

### AlertDialogContent

Centered card with scale and fade transitions.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `portalHost` | `string` | - |
| `size` | `"default" \| "sm"` | `"default"` |

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof AlertDialogPrimitive.Popup>`.

`size` sets the maximum width; `sm` is narrower than `default`. Pass `portalHost` to render into a named portal host. On iOS the content is wrapped in a full-window overlay so it appears above native navigation. Enter and exit animations respect the system Reduce Motion setting.

### AlertDialogHeader

Centered column for the title and description.

Also accepts the props of `ViewProps`.

### AlertDialogFooter

Row of actions with equal-width buttons.

Also accepts the props of `ViewProps`.

Both footer buttons close the dialog and stretch to equal widths.

### AlertDialogMedia

Round badge for an icon above the title.

Also accepts the props of `ViewProps`.

### AlertDialogTitle

Dialog heading.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof AlertDialogPrimitive.Title>`.

### AlertDialogDescription

Supporting text below the title.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof AlertDialogPrimitive.Description>`.

### AlertDialogAction

Button that confirms and closes the dialog.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof AlertDialogPrimitive.Close>`, `React.ComponentProps<typeof Button>`.

### AlertDialogCancel

Secondary button that closes the dialog.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<typeof AlertDialogPrimitive.Close>`, `React.ComponentProps<typeof Button>`.

`AlertDialogAction` and `AlertDialogCancel` accept the `Button` `variant` and `size`. Use `variant="destructive"` on the action for destructive confirmations.

</Only>
