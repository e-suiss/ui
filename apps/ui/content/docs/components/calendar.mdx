`Calendar` is a styled wrapper around `DayPicker` from `react-day-picker`, so it accepts the full `DayPicker` API, including `mode`, `selected`, `onSelect`, `numberOfMonths`, `captionLayout`, `disabled` and `showWeekNumber`. It replaces the day cells with `CalendarDayButton`, which renders each day as a `Button` and styles today, single selections and range start, middle and end.

Use a calendar when people need to see dates in context, such as availability or a stay range. Place it inside a Popover to build a compact date picker; the calendar's background becomes transparent inside popover and card content.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Calendar, CalendarDayButton } from "@/components/ui/calendar"
```

```tsx
<SingleCalendar buttonVariant="plain" />
```

Set `mode` to `single`, `multiple` or `range`, and control the selection with `selected` and `onSelect`. For ranges, `numberOfMonths={2}` shows two months side by side on wider screens.

Set `captionLayout` to `dropdown`, `dropdown-months` or `dropdown-years` to let people jump to a month or year directly; the default is `label`.

Unlike the `DayPicker` default, outside days are hidden unless you pass `showOutsideDays`. The default `locale` is `enUS`. Pass `disabled` with a matcher, such as `{ dayOfWeek: [0, 6] }`, to block days.

Use `CalendarDayButton` when you supply your own `components` and still want the library's day styling.

## Accessibility

- Each month renders as a `grid` labelled with its month and year; selected days set `aria-selected` on their cell.
- Arrow keys move focus between days, and clicking or pressing a day selects it.
- The navigation buttons are labelled for the next and previous month.
- Disabled days render disabled buttons.
- Caption dropdowns are native `select` elements labelled for month and year.

## Notes

- Styling hooks: `data-slot="calendar"` and `data-size` on the root; day buttons expose `data-day`, `data-today`, `data-selected-single`, `data-range-start`, `data-range-middle` and `data-range-end`.
- Cell size and radius come from the `--cell-size` and `--cell-radius` custom properties, which `size` adjusts.
- Pass `classNames` to override any `react-day-picker` class key; your values replace the defaults for that key.
- Navigation chevrons flip in right-to-left layouts.

`Calendar` renders one or more month grids with a caption, previous and next buttons, weekday headers and pressable day cells. Selection mode is set with `mode`: `single`, `multiple` or `range`. Ranges are drawn as a continuous shaded band with filled start and end days.

Three sizes are available: `sm`, `default` and `lg`. The caption can be a plain label or month and year dropdowns built on Select, which suits birth dates and other dates far from today.

## Usage

```tsx
import { CalendarDayButton, Calendar } from "@/components/ui/calendar"
```

```tsx
<SingleCalendar />
```

Pass `mode`, `selected` and `onSelect` to control the selection; `selected` is a `Date`, a `Date[]` or a `{ from, to }` range depending on the mode. Use `month`, `defaultMonth` and `onMonthChange` to control the visible month, and `numberOfMonths` to show several months.

Use `disabled` with date matchers, such as `{ dayOfWeek: [0, 6] }`, to block days. Limit navigation with `startMonth` and `endMonth`; with a year dropdown they default to the last 100 years.

Set `locale` for month and weekday names, and `weekStartsOn` to change the first day of the week. `CalendarDayButton` is exported for custom day rendering.

## Accessibility

- Each month is a `grid` labelled with its caption.
- Day cells have the `button` role, a full date label, and `selected` and `disabled` state.
- On React Native and Android, the previous and next buttons are labelled with the relative month in the calendar's locale, such as "Last month" and "Next month" in English. On iOS they are labelled "Previous month" and "Next month".
- Caption dropdowns are labelled "Choose the Month" and "Choose the Year".

## Notes

- Navigation arrows swap in right-to-left layouts.
- Uses `bg-primary`, `bg-primary/10 dark:bg-primary/20`, `bg-muted` and `text-primary` tokens; light and dark follow the system appearance.
