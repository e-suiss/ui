`Item` is a row container. Inside it, `ItemMedia` holds an icon, image or avatar, `ItemContent` stacks an `ItemTitle` and `ItemDescription`, and `ItemActions` holds buttons or indicators at the end. `ItemHeader` and `ItemFooter` span the full width of the item, above and below the main row. The whole item can also render as a link or button.

`ItemGroup` stacks items as a list, and `ItemSeparator` divides them. Use items to display content; for form controls with labels and validation, use `Field`.

      <ItemDescription>
        Add an extra layer of security to your account.
      </ItemDescription>

## Composition

Use the following composition to build a `Item`:

```text
Item
├── ItemMedia
├── ItemContent
│   ├── ItemTitle
│   └── ItemDescription
├── ItemActions
├── ItemHeader
│   └── ItemTitle
└── ItemFooter
ItemGroup
├── ItemSeparator
└── Item
    ├── ItemMedia
    ├── ItemContent
    │   ├── ItemTitle
    │   └── ItemDescription
    └── ItemActions
```

## Accessibility

- `ItemGroup` has the `list` role; give each `Item` in it the `listitem` role.
- `ItemSeparator` has the `none` role, so it is ignored by assistive technology.
- An item rendered as a link or button is a single focusable element and shows a focus ring.
- Give icon-only action buttons an accessible name with `aria-label`.

## API Reference

### ItemGroup

Stacks items as a list, or groups them into an inset list.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `"default" \| "inset"` | `"default"` |

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

The group has the `list` role, so set `role="listitem"` on each `Item` inside it.

### ItemSeparator

A divider between items.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

### Item

The row container for media, content and actions.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `"default" \| "outline" \| "muted"` | `"default"` |
| `size` | `"default" \| "sm" \| "xs"` | `"default"` |

<OnlyBase base="base">

Also accepts the props of `useRender.ComponentProps<"div">`.

</OnlyBase>

<OnlyBase base="radix">

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `asChild` | `boolean` | `false` |

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

</OnlyBase>

<OnlyBase base="aria">

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `render` | `(props) => React.ReactNode` | - |

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

</OnlyBase>

`Item` sets `data-variant` and `data-size` for styling.

### ItemMedia

Holds an icon, image or avatar at the start of the item.

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `"default" \| "icon" \| "image"` | `"default"` |

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

`icon` sizes an icon, `image` crops an image into a rounded square, and `default` renders the media as is. When `ItemDescription` is present, the media aligns to the top of the item instead of its center.

### ItemContent

Stacks the title and description.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

### ItemTitle

The item's title, limited to one line.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

### ItemDescription

Supporting text, limited to two lines.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"p">`.

### ItemActions

Holds buttons or indicators at the end of the item.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

### ItemHeader

A full-width row above the item's content.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

### ItemFooter

A full-width row below the item's content.

Also accepts the props of `React.ComponentProps<"div">`.

Each part sets a `data-slot`, such as `item-media` or `item-title`, for styling.

`Item` is a row that renders as a `Pressable` with the `button` role when it has `onPress` or `onLongPress`, and as a plain `View` with the `listitem` role otherwise. It has three variants (`default`, `outline`, `muted`) and three sizes (`default`, `sm`, `xs`).

Compose a row from `ItemMedia`, `ItemContent` (holding `ItemTitle` and `ItemDescription`) and `ItemActions`. Use `ItemHeader` and `ItemFooter` for full-width rows above or below the main content.

`ItemGroup` stacks items as a list. Its `inset` variant joins them into one rounded card with square inner corners, which suits settings screens; add `ItemSeparator` between rows to draw an inset divider.

Single-line title.

### ItemDescription

Secondary text, up to two lines.

### ItemActions

Trailing row for values, buttons or chevrons.

### ItemHeader

Full-width row above the content.

Also accepts the props of `ViewProps`.

### ItemFooter
