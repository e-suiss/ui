A horizontally scrolling row of product cards under a two-tone heading, each with an optional kicker, name, tagline, starting price and image. Previous and next buttons sit below the track, and cards shrink to fit narrow screens. With no state of its own, it is a simple base for latest products, featured items or related content rows.
