A dark bento grid of headline numbers: a full-bleed photo tile, two tiles with large gradient figures and a wide charging tile whose `Progress` bar fills when the section loads. Tiles stack on mobile and form a three-column grid on desktop. It always renders dark, whatever the site theme, which suits a dramatic stats or specs band on a product page.

Edit the copy and figures inline and swap the photo in the `image` constant. The bar animates from 0 to 50 shortly after mount; change the target to match your claim.

The photo tile is a CSS background with no text alternative, so keep its heading meaningful. The image is an Unsplash placeholder.
