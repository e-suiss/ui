An invite form that turns typed email addresses into removable chips, assigns a role and optionally sends a welcome email, with pending invitations listed below. Sending shows a toast and moves the addresses to the pending list, where each can be resent or cancelled. It sits in an `AppShell` with admin navigation. Use it for team invites or sharing access with several people at once.

## Notes
