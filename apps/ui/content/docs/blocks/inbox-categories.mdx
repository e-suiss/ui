A mail inbox that sorts messages into Primary, Transactions, Updates and Promotions, switched with icon chips that show a dot when they hold unread mail. Selecting a message opens a reader with reply, star, archive and delete actions and an inline reply box. On mobile the list and reader show one at a time with a back button. Use it for notification centers, categorized inboxes or any feed that needs tabs.

Replace `initialMail` with your messages, each with a sender, subject, body, time, avatar hue, category and unread and starred flags; `categories` sets each chip's icon and tint. Archive and Delete both just remove the message, and the reply form only clears on submit, so connect them to your mail API. The To: Jamie Rivera recipient line is hard-coded.

The reply box receives focus when it opens, and unread dots include hidden Unread text for screen readers.

<Only platform="rn">

A mail screen built on `SplitView`: the list column shows a `ToggleGroup` of four categories with unread dots, and rows with an `Avatar`, sender and preview; the detail column shows the selected message. On wide screens the list and reader sit side by side; on phones the reader opens as its own column. Swipe a row with `SwipeActions` to archive it, or swipe fully to delete. The reader has Reply, Star, Archive and Delete buttons, and Reply opens a `Textarea` to write a response.

Replace `INITIAL_MAIL` with your messages and adjust `CATEGORIES` if you sort mail differently. Wire `onCategoryChange`, `onReply`, `onStarChange`, `onArchive` and `onDelete` to your mail service; the screen updates its own state either way.
