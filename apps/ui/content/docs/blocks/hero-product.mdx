A centered launch hero with an eyebrow, a large product name and tagline, Learn more and Buy buttons, a wide product image and a pricing line. Elements fade and rise in with a slight stagger on load. It is a static server component, ready for a product or feature launch page.

Replace the copy inline and the image URL in the `image` constant. The buttons have no handlers, so wrap them in links to your learn-more and purchase pages, and update the pricing line.

The image is an Unsplash placeholder. Entry animations are skipped when reduced motion is on.
