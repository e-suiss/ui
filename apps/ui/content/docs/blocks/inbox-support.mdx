A help desk inbox with Open, Pending, Solved and All views and live counts, a ticket list with priority badges and a detail pane for replying or marking a ticket solved. Sending a reply moves the ticket to Pending. On mobile the panes show one at a time with a back button. A good base for support queues, moderation queues or any triage workflow.
