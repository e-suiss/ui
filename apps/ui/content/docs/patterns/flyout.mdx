`Flyout` shows supplementary content, such as a small form or a share link, next to the control that opened it. On desktop it renders a `Popover` positioned against the trigger. Below the `48rem` breakpoint it renders a bottom `Drawer` with a swipe handle, and each part switches to its drawer counterpart: `FlyoutHeader`, `FlyoutTitle` and `FlyoutDescription` map to the drawer's header, title and description, and `FlyoutBody` gains padding.

Use it for quick settings or filters that do not need a full modal.

The flyout can be controlled with `open` and `onOpenChange` or uncontrolled with `defaultOpen`. `align`, `alignOffset`, `side` and `sideOffset` on `FlyoutContent` position the desktop popover and are ignored on mobile. Put interactive content in `FlyoutBody`, and use `FlyoutClose` for a button that closes the flyout after an action.

## Accessibility

- The popup has the `dialog` role, named by `FlyoutTitle` and described by `FlyoutDescription`.
- Opening moves focus into the flyout.
- `Escape` closes it and returns focus to the trigger; pressing outside also closes it.

## Notes

Parts carry `data-slot` values from `flyout` through `flyout-close`. `showCloseButton` and `closeLabel` only affect the mobile drawer; on desktop, add a `FlyoutClose` if the popover needs an explicit close control.
