<ComponentPreview type="chart" name="area-chart-stacked-expand" />

Two stacked areas stretched so every month fills the full height, turning absolute numbers into shares. Pick it over the regular stacked chart when the split between series matters more than the total.

Replace `chartData` and rename the series keys in the data, the areas and `chartConfig`. Every series needs the same `stackId` to be normalized together. The tooltip shows the original values, not percentages; add a tooltip formatter if readers need the share. Focus the chart and use the arrow keys to move the tooltip between months.

<Only platform="rn">

Desktop and mobile areas share one stack with `stackOffset="expand"`, so the chart shows the proportion of each series rather than raw totals. A value axis on the right reads 0%, 50% and 100%, and the tooltip lists both series.

Replace `chartData` with your records and change each `dataKey` to match your fields. Rename the series or change their colors in `chartConfig`. The component takes no props.

</Only>
