A stacked bar chart that shows the dashed tooltip indicator, which marks each series with a dashed outline in its color. Compare it with the dot and line variants to pick the indicator that fits your design.

Set `indicator` on `ChartTooltipContent` to `dashed`; it works on any chart. Replace `chartData` and the series in `chartConfig` with your own. The tooltip opens on a preset month when the chart loads; remove `defaultIndex` if it should start hidden.
