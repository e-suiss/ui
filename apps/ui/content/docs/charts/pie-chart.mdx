Pie charts show how a whole splits into a few parts. Use the default pie or a donut for up to five or six slices, put the total in the center of a donut, and label slices directly or with a legend when the names are long. The nested variant shows groups and their members in two rings, and the active and separated variants draw attention to one slice.

Every variant below is its own registry item, so you can add only the ones you need. Each one renders the same design on React, React Native, SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose.
