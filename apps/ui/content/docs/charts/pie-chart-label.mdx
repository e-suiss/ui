A pie with each category name printed outside its slice and connected by a leader line. Use it when readers shouldn't have to match colors against a separate legend.

Replace `chartData` and the category entries in `chartConfig`; labels read their text from the config. Change what the custom `label` renderer returns to print values or percentages, and shrink `outerRadius` if long names get clipped. Outside labels collide when slices are thin, so keep the category count low.

<Only platform="rn kotlin">

A `PieChart` with a smaller radius to leave room for labels. `chartData` holds one record per device with a `users` count. The `label` callback reads each slice name from `chartConfig`, and `labelLine` draws the connector. Pressing a slice shows a tooltip.

</Only>

<Only platform="rn">

Replace `chartData` with your records and keep `dataKey` and `nameKey` in step with your field names. Add one entry per slice to `chartConfig` with its label and color; the component takes no props.

</Only>
