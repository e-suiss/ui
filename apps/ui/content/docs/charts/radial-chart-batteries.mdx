A row of small battery rings, one per device, that switch from green to red when the charge drops to the low threshold. Use compact rings like these for status at a glance, in widgets, settings pages or device lists.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"import { RadialChartBatteries } from \"@/components/charts/radial-chart-batteries\""} />

Replace the `devices` array with your own names and levels from 0 to 100. Change `LOW` to move the warning threshold, and edit the two colors in the per-ring config. The letter in each ring is the first character of the name.

## Notes

The rings are hidden from assistive technology and have no tooltip; the percentage and name printed under each ring carry the information.
