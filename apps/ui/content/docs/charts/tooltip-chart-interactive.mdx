A stacked quarterly revenue chart with live controls for every tooltip option: indicator style, hover or click trigger, a total in the title, and hiding the title or indicator. Use it to try combinations before copying the settings into your own chart.

Copy the props of the combination you like into your chart: `indicator`, `hideLabel` and `hideIndicator` go on the tooltip content, and `trigger` on `ChartTooltip`. The total in the title comes from a label formatter that sums the rows. With the click trigger, the tooltip appears after a bar is clicked and stays in place as the pointer moves. Every control is a labeled switch or toggle group that works with the keyboard.
