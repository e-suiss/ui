An activity summary with a strip of seven day buttons, each showing mini rings, and large Move, Exercise and Stand rings that fill to the selected day's values. Totals and goal progress for each ring update next to it. Use it as a pattern for daily goals that readers browse day by day.

Replace `WEEK` with your days and values, one number per ring in the order of `RINGS`, and edit `RINGS` for labels, units, goals and colors. The `selected` state sets the first selected day. The rings are plain SVG, not a Recharts chart, so there is no tooltip; values beyond the goal show a full ring while the text reports the real percentage. Day buttons work with the keyboard, the totals are announced through a live region, and the fill animation respects reduced motion.

<Only platform="rn">

A `Card` with a `ToggleGroup` of seven days, each showing small rings, above a large ring view and per-ring totals. The rings are drawn with `Svg` circles colored through `ChartPalette`; `RINGS` sets each label, unit, goal and color, and `WEEK` holds three values per day. Picking a day updates the large rings and the totals, which show the value, goal and percentage, with a note when a goal is met.

Replace `WEEK` with your daily values and adjust the goals, units and colors in `RINGS`. The component takes no props.
