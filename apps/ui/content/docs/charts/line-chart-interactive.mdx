Monthly temperatures for several cities with chips to show or hide each city and a switch between average, high and low. A row of stats under the chart shows each city's range and follows the cursor month. Use it as a pattern for comparing many series where readers pick which ones to see.

Replace `CITIES` with your series and add a label and color for each to `chartConfig`; the `visible` state sets which ones start visible. The high and low modes come from a demo formula, so replace `temperature` with real values and fit the fixed value axis `domain` to your range, since values outside it are clipped. The last visible city can't be hidden, and new lines draw in with an animation that is skipped when reduced motion is preferred.

<Only platform="rn">

One line per visible city across twelve months. A `ToggleGroup` switches between average, high and low temperatures, and a row of `Toggle` chips shows or hides each city, keeping at least one on. Touching the plot reports the month through `onActiveIndexChange`, and the summary under the chart switches from each city's range to its value for that month. The lines animate with `animationDuration={650}` and `animationEasing="ease-in-out"`.

Replace `CITIES` with your series and monthly values, and add a matching entry to `chartConfig` and `SWATCH` for each. The high and low values are derived in `temperature()`, so replace that with real data. The component takes no props.
