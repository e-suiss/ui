Two series drawn as grouped bars side by side for each month. Pick it over the stacked variant when readers need to compare the series directly rather than see their total.

Replace `chartData` and rename the series keys in the data, the two `Bar` elements and `chartConfig`, and add a `Bar` for another series. `barGap` sets the space between bars in a group. Beyond three series or a dozen categories, consider stacked bars or a line chart. Focus the chart and use the arrow keys to move the tooltip between months.
