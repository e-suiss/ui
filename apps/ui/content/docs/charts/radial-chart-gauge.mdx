A gauge-style arc that opens at the bottom, showing a single reading with its value and rating in the center. Use it for scores with a known range, such as air quality, health scores or credit utilization.

Set the reading in `chartData` and the scale through the `domain` on `PolarAngleAxis`. The number and rating in the center are static text, so keep them in sync with the value. Change the color in `chartConfig`, or pick it from the value to signal good and bad ranges. There is no tooltip.
