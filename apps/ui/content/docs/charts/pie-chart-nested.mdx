Two concentric rings: a lighter inner pie of group totals surrounded by an outer ring of individual categories. Use it for two-level breakdowns, such as product lines and the products within them.

Put the detailed records in `chartData` and the group totals in `groups`, with each group total equal to the sum of its categories. Order the outer categories so each group's members sit next to their inner slice. The demo groups reuse the Phone and Watch keys, so their tooltips read Phone and Watch; give each group its own key and label in `chartConfig`.

<Only platform="rn kotlin">

A `PieChart` with two `Pie` layers. The inner pie reads a `groups` array and is drawn at reduced opacity; the outer ring reads `chartData`, one record per device. Pressing a slice in either ring shows a tooltip.

</Only>

<Only platform="rn">

Replace `groups` and `chartData` with your summary and detail records, and add each name to `chartConfig` with its label and color. The component takes no props.

</Only>
