One series drawn as a filled shape across six axes on a polygon grid. Use it to show the profile of a single item across several comparable scores, such as skills or product ratings.

Replace `chartData` with one record per axis, and set `dataKey` on `PolarAngleAxis` to the axis name field and on `Radar` to your value field. Rename the series in `chartConfig` to set its label and color. The radius adapts to the largest value, so two charts with different data aren't directly comparable; the radius-axis variant uses a fixed range. Radar charts read best with five to eight axes.

The basic `RadarChart`: a polygon `PolarGrid`, an angle axis for each skill and one filled `Radar` series. `chartData` has one record per axis (`skill`) with `thisMonth` and `lastMonth` scores. Only `thisMonth` is drawn. Pressing the chart shows a tooltip.

<Only platform="rn">

Replace `chartData` with your records, set `PolarAngleAxis` `dataKey` to your axis field, and rename the series in `chartConfig`. The component takes no props.

</Only>
