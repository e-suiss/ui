A donut with a hollow center and small gaps between slices. It reads lighter than a full pie and leaves the center free for a label if you need one later.

Replace `chartData` and add a label and color for each category key to `chartConfig`. `innerRadius` and `outerRadius` set the ring thickness, and `paddingAngle` the gap between slices.

<Only platform="rn kotlin">

A `PieChart` whose `Pie` uses `innerRadius` to leave the middle empty and `paddingAngle` to space the slices. `chartData` holds one record per device with a `users` count. Pressing a slice shows a tooltip.

</Only>

<Only platform="rn">

Replace `chartData` with your records and keep `dataKey` and `nameKey` in step with your field names. Add one entry per slice to `chartConfig` with its label and color; the component takes no props.

</Only>
