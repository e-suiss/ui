A single smooth line with a dot and a printed value at every point and no grid. Choose it for short series where readers want the exact numbers without hovering.

Replace `chartData`, point each `dataKey` at your fields and set the label and color in `chartConfig`. The `position` prop on `LabelList` moves the value labels; raise the chart's top margin if they get clipped. Labels overlap on long series, so keep it to a dozen points or fewer.

One natural curve with dots on each month and a `LabelList` above every point, so values are readable without touching the chart. There is no grid; a month axis and a tooltip remain.

Replace `chartData` with your records and change each `dataKey` to match your fields. Change `position` and `offset` on the `LabelList` to move the labels. The component takes no props.
