Monthly cash flow where bars rise green above a zero line and drop red below it. Use it for any value that can be negative, such as profit, net change or deltas against a target.

Replace `chartData` and rename `flow` in the data, the bar and `chartConfig`. Each `Cell` picks its color from the sign of the value, so change the two colors there, and `interval` on the axis sets how many month labels show. Because colors are set per bar, the tooltip has no swatch, so keep the zero line to show the direction without color. Focus the chart and use the arrow keys to move the tooltip between months.

<Only platform="rn">

One bar per month, colored per value through a `Cell`: green when the flow is positive and red when it is negative. A `ReferenceLine` at zero anchors the bars, and the month axis shows every other label.

Replace `chartData` with your months and signed `flow` values. Change the colors in the `Cell` `fill` expression. The component takes no props.
