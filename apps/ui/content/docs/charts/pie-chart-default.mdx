A full pie split into five slices separated by thin gaps, with a tooltip for each slice. Use it for a quick part-to-whole view of a small number of categories.

Give each record a category key, a value and a `fill` that references its color, and add a label and color for every key to `chartConfig`. Point `dataKey` and `nameKey` on the `Pie` at your value and category fields. Pies work best with about five slices or fewer; group the rest into an Other slice.

<Only platform="rn kotlin">

The simplest `PieChart`: one `Pie` inside a `Card`, with slices separated by a thin stroke in the card color. `chartData` holds one record per device with a `users` count. Pressing a slice shows a tooltip with its name and value.

</Only>

<Only platform="rn">

Replace `chartData` with your records and keep `dataKey` and `nameKey` in step with your field names. Add one entry per slice to `chartConfig` with its label and color; the component takes no props.

</Only>
