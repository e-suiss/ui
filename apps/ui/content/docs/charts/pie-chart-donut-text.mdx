A donut with the total shown in its center, so readers get the overall number and the breakdown in one glance. Prefer it over the plain donut when the total is the headline.

Replace `chartData` and the category entries in `chartConfig`; the center total is summed from the data. Change the caption under the number to your unit, and adjust `innerRadius` if a longer number needs more room. The center text lets pointer events through, so slice tooltips still work.

<Only platform="rn">

A `PieChart` donut with small gaps and rounded slice corners. `chartData` holds one record per device with a `users` count. The total is summed from `chartData` and drawn as `Text` layered over the ring. Pressing a slice shows a tooltip.

Replace `chartData` with your records; the center total recalculates from it. Update the `users` caption and `chartConfig` labels and colors to match. The component takes no props.

</Only>
