One rounded bar per month with a `LabelList` placed at the top of each bar, so values are readable without touching the chart. There is no grid; a month axis and a tooltip remain.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"import { BarChartLabeled } from \"@/components/charts/bar-chart-labeled\""} />

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<BarChartLabeled />"} />

Replace `chartData` with your records and change each `dataKey` to match your fields. Change `position` and `offset` on the `LabelList` to move the labels. The component takes no props.
