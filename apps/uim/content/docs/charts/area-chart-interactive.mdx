One price series drawn as a linear area with a fading gradient, a value axis on the right and a dashed `ReferenceLine` at the opening price. A `ToggleGroup` switches between 1D, 1W, 1M, 3M, 1Y and 5Y. Touching the plot reports the point through `onActiveIndexChange`, and the header updates the price, change and date for that point. The area turns green or red depending on whether the price is up or down, and open, high, low and volume sit below the chart.
