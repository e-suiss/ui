A `RadialBarChart` drawn from 180 to 0 degrees, with two `RadialBar` series sharing a `stackId` on a domain equal to their sum. `chartData` is a single record with `desktop` and `mobile` counts, and the total is `Text` under the arc. Pressing the arc shows a tooltip.
