A `Card` with a row of `Toggle` chips, a `RadarChart` and a summary card per model. Scores live in `SCORES`, one array per model, in the order of `AXES`. Up to three models can be selected; picking a fourth drops the oldest, and the last one cannot be cleared. Pressing an axis bolds its label and switches each summary from the overall average to that axis score; a tooltip lists the values.
