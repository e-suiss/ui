Bars stack social, productivity and entertainment time. A `ToggleGroup` switches between a weekly view and an hourly view of one day. In the week view, touching a bar selects that day through `onActiveIndexChange`, dims the other days and updates the headline, comparison text, category totals and the most used apps list below. A dashed line marks the weekly average.
