A `RadarChart` that adds a `PolarRadiusAxis` with five ticks over a fixed 0 to 100 domain, plus one filled `Radar` series. `chartData` has one record per axis (`skill`) with `thisMonth` and `lastMonth` scores. Pressing the chart shows a tooltip.
