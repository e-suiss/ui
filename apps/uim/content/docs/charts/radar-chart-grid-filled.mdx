A `RadarChart` whose circular `PolarGrid` is filled with the control color and separated by strokes in the card color. The single `Radar` series has no stroke. `chartData` has one record per axis (`skill`) with `thisMonth` and `lastMonth` scores. Pressing the chart shows a tooltip.
