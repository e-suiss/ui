A `Card` with a `ToggleGroup` that switches between phone and cloud storage, a stacked usage bar, a `PieChart` donut and a list of categories. Sizes come from the `STORAGE` record, and free space is the total minus what is used. Pressing a slice or a list row selects that category: its slice lifts outward, other segments in the bar dim, and the center text shows its size and share. Pressing it again returns to total used.
