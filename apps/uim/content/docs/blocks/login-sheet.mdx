An app welcome screen with two buttons: Sign in with suiss and Continue with email. The first opens a `Drawer` that shows the account name and a `RadioGroup` to share the real email or use a hidden forwarding address, with a Continue `Button` that shows a `Spinner` before closing the sheet and showing a welcome message.
