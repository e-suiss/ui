A sign-in screen that shows the account with an `Avatar`, a Change link, a Continue with passkey `Button` and a link to sign in with a password. Continuing opens a `Dialog` with an animated scan glyph that turns into a verified state, then shows a welcome view with a Sign out link. The dialog can be cancelled during the scan.
