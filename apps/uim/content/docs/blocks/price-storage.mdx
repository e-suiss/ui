A pricing screen with a `ToggleGroup` for the billing period and a `RadioGroup` of plan cards, each with a size, price and feature list. The selected card lifts slightly, and the most popular plan carries a label. Yearly prices show ten months of the monthly price. A `BottomBar` `Button` confirms the switch.
