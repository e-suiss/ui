A sign-in screen inside a `KeyboardScrollView`. The email `Input` comes first; when the address looks valid, the password field appears below it and takes focus. Invalid input shakes the form and shows an error. It also has a Remember me `Checkbox` with a `Label`, a Forgot password link, a create account link, and a `Spinner` while signing in, followed by a welcome view with an `Avatar` and a Sign out link.
