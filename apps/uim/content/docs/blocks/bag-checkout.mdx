A checkout built from `Screen`, `Header` with a collapsing large title, `RadioGroup`, `Button`, `Icon` and `BottomBar`. Delivery and payment are chosen with radio options, finished steps collapse to a one-line summary with an Edit link, and the bottom bar button advances through the steps. The total updates when express delivery is picked, and placing the order shows a confirmation screen with a Start over link.
