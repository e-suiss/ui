Composes `Screen`, `Header`, `DropdownMenu` for picking the store, `ToggleGroup`, the `BarChart` from `Chart`, `Chip` filters, `Avatar`, `Item` rows and `TabBar`. The bar chart compares online and in-store sales per day, and pressing a day highlights it and shows its total. Order status chips filter the list.
