A product screen with a hero photo per device, a `ToggleGroup` to pick the device, and a `RadioGroup` of payment options. For the phone, a `Switch` adds theft and loss protection. A `BottomBar` shows the total, which updates with each choice, and an Add to Bag `Button`.
