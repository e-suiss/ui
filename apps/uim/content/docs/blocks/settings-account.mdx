The main page shows an `Avatar` and name above an inset `ItemGroup` of account sections; tapping one pushes it into the same `Header` with a back button. The Sign-In & Security page has cards for email and phone that open an inline `Input` with Save and Cancel, a passkeys `Switch`, and a confirmation that appears after saving. Other sections show a placeholder.
