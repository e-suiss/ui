Composes `Screen`, `Header`, `SwipeActions` from the interactions, `Avatar`, `Message`, `Bubble`, `MessageScroller`, `InputGroup`, `Button` and `BottomBar`. Tapping a row opens the thread with a back button, video call and details actions, and a composer. Sending a message shows a typing indicator followed by a sample reply. Swiping a row reveals a Delete action.
