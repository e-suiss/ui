Composes `Screen`, `Stepper` from the interactions, `Button`, `Text` and `BottomBar`. Each line has a quantity stepper, a product details link and a Remove button, and the heading and totals update as the bag changes. Removing every item shows an empty state with a Continue Shopping button.
