A hero image sits above an `InputAccessoryBar` with email and password inputs; the password shows a five-level strength meter as you type. A `Checkbox` with a `Label` accepts the terms, and the Start free trial `Button` in the `BottomBar` stays disabled until the form is valid, then shows a welcome screen. A sign-in link sits below.
