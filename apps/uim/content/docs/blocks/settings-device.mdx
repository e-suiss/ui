A large-title `Header` sits over an `InputGroup` search field, an account `Item` with an `Avatar`, and inset `ItemGroup` lists of rows with tinted icons. Airplane Mode, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Focus use a `Switch`, and other rows show a detail value and a chevron. Typing filters the rows and shows a message when nothing matches.
