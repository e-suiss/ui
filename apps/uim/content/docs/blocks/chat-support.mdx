Composes `Screen`, `Message`, `Bubble`, `MessageScroller`, `InputGroup`, `Button`, `Icon` and `BottomBar`. The chat panel opens and closes with a scale animation from a launcher button, quick questions disappear once asked, and each question gets a typed answer after a short typing indicator. The panel grows to the available height while the composer has focus.
