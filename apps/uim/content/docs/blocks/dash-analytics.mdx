Composes `Screen`, `Header`, `DropdownMenu` for picking the app, `ToggleGroup`, the `AreaChart` from `Chart`, `Item` rows and `TabBar`. Switching the metric or app redraws the chart, and dragging across the chart updates the headline value for that week. The other tabs show a placeholder section.
