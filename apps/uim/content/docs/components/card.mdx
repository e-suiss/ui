`Card` is a rounded `View` that stacks a `CardHeader`, `CardContent` and `CardFooter` with consistent spacing. The `default` variant is a raised surface with a hairline border and a shadow; the `filled` variant is a flat secondary surface.

The `sm` size reduces the padding and gaps for dense layouts. All parts read the size from context, so their horizontal padding stays aligned.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"import { Card, CardHeader, CardTitle, CardDescription, CardAction, CardContent, CardFooter } from \"@/components/ui/card\""} />

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<Card className=\"w-full\">\n  <CardHeader>\n    <CardTitle>Login to your account</CardTitle>\n    <CardDescription>\n      Enter your email below to login to your account\n    </CardDescription>\n  </CardHeader>\n  <CardContent className=\"gap-4\">\n    <View className=\"gap-2\">\n      <Label nativeID=\"card-email\">Email</Label>\n      <Input\n        accessibilityLabelledBy=\"card-email\"\n        placeholder=\"m@example.com\"\n        keyboardType=\"email-address\"\n        autoCapitalize=\"none\"\n      />\n    </View>\n    <View className=\"gap-2\">\n      <Label nativeID=\"card-password\">Password</Label>\n      <Input accessibilityLabelledBy=\"card-password\" secureTextEntry />\n    </View>\n  </CardContent>\n  <CardFooter className=\"flex-col gap-2\">\n    <Button block>\n      <Text>Login</Text>\n    </Button>\n    <Button block variant=\"secondary\">\n      <Text>Login with Google</Text>\n    </Button>\n  </CardFooter>\n</Card>"} />

Put a `CardTitle` and `CardDescription` in `CardHeader`. Add a `CardAction` anywhere in the header and it is placed in the top-end corner beside the title.

To show an image edge to edge, place it directly in the card before the header and add `pt-0` to the card; only the header, content and footer are padded horizontally.

## Accessibility

- `CardTitle` has the `heading` role.
- The card itself adds no role; actions inside provide their own.

## Notes

- Uses `bg-surface-raised` with a shadow, or `bg-surface-secondary` for `filled`; the border is stronger in dark mode.
- Light and dark follow the system appearance.
