`Collapsible` is an unstyled root with a `CollapsibleTrigger` and a `CollapsibleContent` panel. It handles the open state and accessibility, and leaves the look to you.

Use it for a single show-more section. For a list of sections where opening one can close another, use Accordion.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<CollapsibleDemo initialOpen />"} />

Put any content in `CollapsibleTrigger`, for example a `Text` and a caret `Icon`, and use `render` to render it as a `Button`. Use `open` with `onOpenChange` for controlled state, or `defaultOpen` for uncontrolled.

Set `keepMounted` on `CollapsibleContent` to keep the content mounted while closed.

## Accessibility

- The trigger has the `button` role with `expanded` and `disabled` state.
- Closed content is hidden from VoiceOver and TalkBack.
