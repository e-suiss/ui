`Header` is a 44-point bar that sorts its children into three slots: `HeaderBack` and leading `HeaderActions` on the start side, `HeaderTitle` in the center, and everything else on the end side. The side slots share the remaining width equally, so the title stays centered.

For a large title, create a scroll state with `useHeaderScroll()`, pass it to both `Header` and `HeaderScrollView`, and put a `HeaderLargeTitle` at the top of the scroll view. As the large title scrolls away, the small title fades in and a hairline appears under the bar. Pulling down stretches the large title slightly.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"import { Header, HeaderTitle, HeaderLargeTitle, HeaderBack, HeaderActions, HeaderScrollView } from \"@/components/ui/header\""} />

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<View className=\"-mx-4 -mt-4\">\n  <Header>\n    <HeaderBack>Settings</HeaderBack>\n    <HeaderTitle>Profile</HeaderTitle>\n    <HeaderActions>\n      <Button variant=\"plain\" size=\"sm\">\n        <Text className=\"font-semibold\">Edit</Text>\n      </Button>\n    </HeaderActions>\n  </Header>\n</View>"} />

`HeaderBack` shows a caret and an optional text label such as the previous screen's name; wire `onPress` to your navigation. Group icon buttons in `HeaderActions`, and set `side="leading"` to place a group on the start side.

Both `HeaderTitle` and `HeaderLargeTitle` accept a `subtitle`. Plain strings are wrapped in `Text` and truncated to one line in the bar.

Without a large title, you can still pass the same `scroll` to `Header` and `HeaderScrollView` to show the hairline once content scrolls under the bar.

## Accessibility

- `HeaderTitle` and a string `HeaderLargeTitle` have the `heading` role.
- `HeaderBack` has the `button` role and is labelled with its text, or "Back" when it has none.
- Give icon-only actions an `accessibilityLabel`.

## Notes

- The back caret rotates and the large title scales from the correct edge in right-to-left layouts.
- Uses `text-link`, `bg-separator` and `bg-item-pressed` tokens; light and dark follow the system appearance.
