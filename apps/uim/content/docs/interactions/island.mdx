`Island` floats below the top safe area and morphs between views: `hidden`, `compact`, `expanded` and `notice`. Its width, height and corner radius spring to the measured size of the active view, and parts cross-fade as it changes.

It appears in compact form while an `IslandCompact` is mounted. Pressing it expands to `IslandExpanded`; pressing outside, swiping up or the Android back button collapses it. `notify` shows a notice that replaces the current view and dismisses itself after 4 seconds, on press or on swipe up. Use it for ongoing activity such as a download, timer or call.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"import { IslandProvider, Island, IslandCompact, IslandExpanded, IslandRing } from \"@/components/interactions/island\""} />

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<IslandProvider>\n  <DownloadsExample initial={0.42} />\n</IslandProvider>"} />

Wrap the app in `IslandProvider` and render one `Island` with an `IslandCompact` and an `IslandExpanded` as children. Mount the compact part only while there is activity to show.

`useIsland` returns `view`, `expanded`, `setExpanded`, `notify` and `dismiss`. A notice is `{ icon?, title, description?, duration? }`; pass a `duration` of `0` or `Infinity` to keep it until dismissed. Dragging the island pauses the notice timer. Use `IslandRing` for circular progress. The `IslandNotice` and `IslandView` types are exported.

## Accessibility

- Notices are announced with their title and description when they are text.
- The compact view is a button that exposes `expanded` and has an `expand` action.
- The expanded view has the `group` role, a `collapse` action and closes on the escape gesture.
- The notice has a `dismiss` action.
- Inactive views and the progress ring are hidden from screen readers.

## Notes

- The island uses `bg-label` with `text-surface` in light mode, and `bg-surface` with a `border-separator` outline in dark mode.
- Expanded and notice views are up to 384 points wide.
- Size and fade animations respect the system reduce motion setting.
