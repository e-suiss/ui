`SelectionList` switches a list between normal and editing modes. In editing mode each `SelectionListItem` slides in a check circle at its start, the whole row toggles on press, and selected rows are highlighted.

`SelectionListBar` floats above the bottom safe area while editing, sliding up into view, for actions such as delete or move. Leaving editing mode clears the selection. On Android the hardware back button also leaves it. Use it for mail, notes or photo lists that need bulk actions.

Wrap the list in `SelectionList`, add a `SelectionListTrigger` that toggles editing (it reads `Select` and `Done` by default), and give each `SelectionListItem` a unique `value`. Put your buttons and a `SelectionListCount` in `SelectionListBar`.

Editing and the selection can be uncontrolled with `defaultEditing` and `defaultValue`, or controlled with `editing`, `onEditingChange`, `value` and `onValueChange`. `useSelectionList` returns `editing`, `selected`, `toggle`, `selectAll` and `clear` for custom actions. Outside editing mode, the row's children receive presses as usual.

## Accessibility

- While editing, rows have the `checkbox` role and expose their `checked` state; otherwise the row wrapper is not focusable and its children are.
- The trigger exposes a `selected` state while editing.
- The bar has the `toolbar` role.
- `SelectionListCount` is a polite live region, reading `Select items` or `n selected` by default.

## Notes

- Selected rows use `bg-item-selected`; checked circles use `bg-accent` with an `on-accent` check.
- The bar uses `bg-surface-raised`, or `bg-surface-secondary` in dark mode, and is up to `max-w-md` wide.
- Animations respect the system reduce motion setting.
