A protection plan picker: choose a device, pick a payment option for it and, for some devices, add theft and loss cover, with a live total and an Add to Bag button. A photo of the selected device sits beside the options on desktop and above them on mobile. Use it for warranties, insurance add-ons or any per-product plan.
