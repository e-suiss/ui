Two overlapping series whose fills fade to transparent toward the baseline, so the lower series stays visible through the upper one. Reach for it over the flat-filled default when you compare two series that cross each other.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"import { AreaChartGradient } from \"@/components/charts/area-chart-gradient\""} />

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<AreaChartGradient />"} />

Replace `chartData` and rename the two series keys consistently across the data, the areas and `chartConfig`. Each series needs its own gradient in the `defs` block, so add or rename one when you add a series. Raise or lower the top stop's `stopOpacity` to make the fill stronger or subtler.

## Notes

The series are drawn in source order, so the last `Area` sits on top. Focus the plot and use the arrow keys to move the tooltip between months.
