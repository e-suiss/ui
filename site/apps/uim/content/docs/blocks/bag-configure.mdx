Composes `Screen`, `ToggleGroup` color swatches, `Button`, `Text` and `BottomBar`. Model and storage are radio-style options, the finish swatches cross-fade the product photo, and the bottom bar shows the price and a monthly amount that update as you choose. Add to Bag switches its label to Added to Bag.
