A horizontal `BarChart` whose `range` values are `[low, high]` pairs, so each bar floats between the two temperatures. Bars use a teal-to-orange gradient over a full-width track, day names sit on the left and highs on the right. The tooltip shows the range in degrees.

## Usage

<Code lang="tsx" code={"import { BarChartRange } from \"@/components/charts/bar-chart-range\""} />

<Code lang="tsx" code={"<BarChartRange />"} />

Replace `chartData` with your days and `low` and `high` values; the `range` pair is derived from them. Adjust the hidden `XAxis` `domain` to fit your temperature range. The component takes no props.
